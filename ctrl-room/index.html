The Idea

A production-camp tour created by Diiamoond to bring producers, songwriters and creatives together in the same room.

To create, exchange ideas and push music forward.

/ 05

The Room

Built around the belief that producers grow through collaboration, community and shared knowledge. CTRL ROOM takes the traditional production camp and turns it into an immersive experience. Instead of working individually, producers come together to exchange:

  • Sounds
  • Production techniques
  • Creative ideas
  • Workflows
  • Samples
  • Melodies
  • Industry experience

/ 05

The Tour

Launched in Europe.

A community of producers from different countries, backgrounds and musical influences. Each camp brings established producers and emerging talent together to connect, build relationships and create music.

/ 05

The Philosophy

Control through creativity.

Producers take control of their sound, their network and their development. It is not only about making records: it is a community where knowledge moves freely between producers.

Through CTRL ROOM, Diiamoond is building a platform for producers to connect, collaborate and grow together, bringing different scenes and sounds into the same room.
