Origins

Diiamoond is an Italy-born music producer, executive producer, beatmaker and songwriter whose career has grown from the foundations of church music into an international journey spanning Afrobeats, hip-hop, pop and contemporary music.

Born in Italy in 2003, Diiamoond developed a passion for music from an early age. Raised in the church, he grew up surrounded by music and developed a natural appreciation for rhythm, melody, songwriting and the emotional power of sound. As his passion evolved, he became particularly drawn to Afrobeats and hip-hop, eventually turning his love for music into a career in production.

/ 09

The Foundation

Diiamoond began his professional journey in Italy, where he started developing his signature sound and building relationships within the music industry.

His creativity and versatility soon opened doors to collaborations with artists from different backgrounds and musical scenes. Among his early and notable collaborations were artists such as Kap G, K CAMP, Tommy Kuti and Slings, helping establish Diiamoond as a young producer with an increasingly international outlook.

/ 09

Beyond Italy

His work soon began travelling beyond Italy, reaching the United Kingdom and other international markets.

With an ability to move naturally between different sounds and cultures, Diiamoond continued expanding his catalogue while developing a reputation for his strong work ethic, creative instincts and understanding of the modern music landscape.

/ 09

The UK

A significant chapter in his career came with his move to the United Kingdom, where he began making an impact within the English pop and urban music scene.

One of his notable UK credits came through his work on Mimi Webb’s “24/5,” featured on her Seven Shades of Heartbreak project. The track received strong radio attention and reached the Top 10 of the UK Radio Charts, marking another important step in Diiamoond’s growing international career.

/ 09

The Catalogue

His catalogue has continued to expand across genres and markets, with production and songwriting credits connected to artists including Mimi Webb, Kap G, K CAMP, GoldLink, Shakka, Tommy Kuti, Yves the Male and Slings, among others.

Beyond Music

Beyond his work in music, Diiamoond has continued to expand his creative presence into the wider entertainment industry.

His recent work with Netflix represents another evolution in his career, bringing his production and creative expertise into the world of global film and television.

Placement: Luther: The Fallen Sun (Netflix, 2023)

/ 09

The Sound

What distinguishes Diiamoond’s journey is the breadth of his musical path.

From growing up around church music in Italy, to discovering his passion for Afrobeats and hip-hop, to working across Italy, the UK and international markets, his career has been built around the idea that music can cross borders and connect different cultures.

Still developing his sound and expanding his catalogue, Diiamoond represents a new generation of producers whose work exists at the intersection of African music, European creativity and global pop culture. With Gold and Platinum records already part of his story, major international collaborations behind him, and new opportunities across music and entertainment ahead, Diiamoond continues to build his legacy behind the sound.
